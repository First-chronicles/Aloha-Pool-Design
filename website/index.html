Pool Tips

What should you know before building a pool?

Short answers to what homeowners ask us most, straight from how we build.

Construction

What happens on shoot day?

Shoot day is when the pool shell is sprayed with shotcrete over the steel. It is the first day the pool really looks like a pool.

Read the Tip

Before shoot day the steel, plumbing and electrical are already in place. Shotcrete is sprayed over the steel to form the shell, including the spa and any benches or steps.

Then the shell needs water. The homeowner waters it down several times a day for 7 to 10 days while it cures. After that, coping and tile go on.

Planning

Why landscaping usually goes in before plaster

Planting and grading are messy. Doing them before plaster keeps dirt out of a fresh pool finish.

Read the Tip

Plaster is the last step in building the pool, and the pool starts filling the day it is plastered. Anything that blows or washes in after that has to be cleaned out.

That is why we usually install landscaping after the deck and before plaster. It can still be done after the pool is finished, or by the homeowner, if that works better for you.

Ownership

What start-up looks like with Aloha

Filling the pool is not the end of the job. Start-up is when you learn to run it.

Read the Tip

Once the pool is plastered and full, we set a time with you to get it going. We program the equipment, then balance and clean the water.

For the next four weeks we keep coming out to care for the pool and show you how to do it, so you are comfortable on your own.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free. The owner meets you at your home to talk through the space and how you want to use it, then draws up a sketch and comes back for a second meeting to go over the design and pricing.

Will I work with a salesperson?

No. The owner of Aloha Pool & Design works directly with you from concept to completion. There are no salespeople and no middleman, and you have direct contact with the owner the whole time.

Is Aloha Pool & Design licensed?

Yes. Aloha Pool & Design Inc holds California C-53 Swimming Pool Contractor license #965927 with the Contractors State License Board. You can check the license on the CSLB website any time.

Can you handle the concrete, landscaping and patio cover too?

Yes. Concrete, landscaping, patio covers and design services are provided through our sister company, Aloha Landscape, so one team can take care of the pool and the yard around it.

What are the steps to build an in-ground pool?

A typical Aloha pool and spa build goes: design, dig day, steel, plumbing, electrical, shoot day (shotcrete), coping and tile, decking, landscaping, plaster, and start-up. Demolition comes first if the yard needs it.

Do I have to do anything during construction?

A little. After shoot day the homeowner waters the new shell several times a day for 7 to 10 days while it cures, and the new deck is watered for about a week. During plumbing you can also tell the plumber where you want the spa's therapy jets.

When should the landscaping go in?

Usually before plaster, so any dirt or mess from planting cannot land in a freshly plastered pool. Landscaping can also be done after the pool is finished, or by the homeowner.

What happens after the pool is filled?

We schedule start-up with you, set the equipment programs, and balance and clean the water. Then we come out for the next four weeks to keep caring for the pool while teaching you how to do it yourself.

What areas do you serve?
